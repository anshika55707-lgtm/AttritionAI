Model Performance Interpretation

The model's accuracy indicates its overall prediction performance. Precision measures how many predicted attrition cases were correct, while recall measures how many actual attrition cases were identified. The F1 Score balances precision and recall, and ROC-AUC measures the model's ability to distinguish between employees who leave and employees who stay.
